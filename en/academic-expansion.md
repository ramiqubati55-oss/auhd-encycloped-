# Academic Expansion — Faculties and Programs

[Home](../index.html) | [العربية](../ar/academic-expansion.md)

Azal University for Human Development, based in Sana'a, Yemen, offers academic pathways in health sciences, engineering, technology, business, humanities, special education and postgraduate studies.

## Faculties
- **Medical Sciences:** Applied health education including pharmacy, nursing, midwifery, anesthesia technology, medical imaging, physical therapy and clinical nutrition.
- **Engineering and Information Technology:** Civil and architectural engineering, information technology, cybersecurity, graphics and multimedia.
- **Administrative and Human Sciences:** Business administration, international business, accounting, management information systems, health administration, English language and translation.
- **Special Education:** Special education, psychological and educational counseling, and related disciplines.

## Master's Programs
- Business Administration (MBA)
- Information Technology
- Health and Hospital Management
- Public Health
- Midwifery
- Clinical Psychology
- Special Education
