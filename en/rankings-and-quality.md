# International Presence, Rankings and Institutional Recognition

[Home](../index.html) | [العربية](../ar/rankings-and-quality.md)

Azal University for Human Development maintains an international academic and professional presence through institutional memberships, university directories, research indicators and academic quality initiatives.

## International Memberships and Affiliations

- **International Pharmaceutical Federation (FIP):** The university appears in the [FIP World List of Pharmacy Schools](https://www.fip.org/world-list-of-pharmacy-schools?item=3749).
- **United Nations Academic Impact (UNAI):** The university is listed in the [UN Academic Impact membership directory](https://www.un.org/sites/un2.un.org/files/unai_master_members_list.pdf).

## University Rankings and Research Indicators

- **EduRank:** [Azal University profile](https://edurank.org/uni/azal-university-of-human-development/).
- **AD Scientific Index:** According to the university's reported 2025 figures, AUHD placed **fourth among private Yemeni universities** and **ninth nationally** in the research and publication indicator. [Yemen university rankings](https://www.adscientificindex.com/university-ranking/?country_code=ye).
- **uniRank:** [AUHD institutional listing](https://www.unirank.org/ye/uni/azal-university-of-human-development/).
- **Webometrics:** [Yemen university directory and ranking](https://www.webometrics.info/en/Asia/Yemen).

## Accreditation and Professional Services

- **Yemeni higher education authorities:** [Private university admissions portal](https://oasyemen.net/private/admission) and [Council for Accreditation and Quality Assurance](https://caqa.gov.ye/alakhbar/topic/242).
- **Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS):** [Official website](https://scfhs.org.sa/ar) and [inquiry channels](https://scfhs.org.sa/ar/tawasul) for professional classification and registration procedures.
